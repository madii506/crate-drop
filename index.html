02 — How the crate drops

They promised. We drop.

1

Open your crate

Paste any wallet. We add up the fees it paid on pump.fun and PumpSwap, read from each trade on the chain.

2

Register

At launch, sign one free message to put your wallet in the snapshot. No transaction, no approvals.

3

Claim

The whole dev buy goes into a public claimable airdrop, weighted by the fees you paid. Whatever is left after 30 days is burned.

4

SOL every hour

A public bot claims $CRATE’s creator fees every hour and sends the SOL to registered holders. Every payout is linked.

Who gets the most

  • Weighted by the fees your wallet paid to pump.fun.
  • Capped per wallet, so whales can’t take it all.
  • Only fees paid before the snapshot count, so it can’t be farmed after today.

What we never do

  • Ask for a seed phrase or private key.
  • DM you about a claim.
  • Ask you to sign anything except the free register message and your claim.

04 — FAQ

Questions.

Is this pump.fun’s airdrop?

No. CRATE is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun. It’s a memecoin doing the thing they promised: paying back the people who paid the fees.

How is my share decided?

By the fees your wallet paid to pump.fun on the bonding curve and on PumpSwap before the snapshot, capped per wallet. The same weighting is used for the token claim and the hourly SOL.

Do I need to connect my wallet to open my crate?

No. Paste any address. We only read public chain data.

Why does it only read my recent trades?

Free Solana RPCs limit how much history one request can read, so the checker reads your latest transactions. The launch snapshot reads full histories.

Where does the hourly SOL come from?

From $CRATE’s creator fees. A public bot claims them every hour and sends them out. Every payout is posted with its Solscan link.

Someone DMed me a claim link. Is it real?

No. Claims only happen on this site. Nobody from CRATE will DM you, and nobody will ever ask for your seed phrase.
